Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

RIFT: World Rush is an arcade game by AN GAMES. It has no account or sign-in and works offline. This policy explains the two things in the game that involve third parties: advertising and in-app purchases.

Advertising

The free version shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation, which may serve ads from Google AdMob, Unity Ads and other partner networks. Rewarded ads are only shown when you choose to watch one; a short ad also appears after each round you finish, whether you win or lose.

On first launch you choose between personalised ads and basic ads. With personalised ads, and only if you also allow tracking in Apple's or Google's system prompt, the ad networks may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA / Google Advertising ID), coarse location derived from IP address, device model and OS version, and ad interaction data to select and measure ads. With basic ads the same networks receive only what is technically required to serve an ad (IP address, device type) and do not use your advertising identifier for personalisation. You can change your choice at any time in Settings inside the game, or reset your advertising identifier in your device settings.

Our ad partners process this data under their own policies: Unity / ironSource. We do not receive personal data from them; we see only aggregated revenue and impression statistics.

Purchases

Purchases (Remove Ads Forever, skins) are handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. The game stores a local record that a purchase was made so it can unlock the item, and can restore it from your store account on a new device.

What we do not do

We do not collect names, e-mail addresses, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal data, and we do not sell data. Game progress is stored only on your device.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Ad networks are configured accordingly.
