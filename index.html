Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

RIFT: World Rush is an arcade game by AN GAMES. There is no sign-in and no password, and the game plays offline. This policy explains the three things that involve anyone else: advertising, in-app purchases, and the support service the game registers with so we can help you if you write in.

Advertising

The free version shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation. LevelPlay selects between ad networks on our behalf. Every network that may receive data: ironSource and Google AdMob, which serve today, and Unity Ads, whose adapter is included in the app but whose network is not switched on, so nothing is served from it. It is listed because it ships inside the app and could be turned on. We update this page before any network begins serving. Rewarded ads are only shown when you choose to watch one, and a short ad appears after a round you finish. The one-time Remove Ads Forever purchase removes those between-round ads permanently.

On first launch you choose between personalised ads and basic ads. With personalised ads, the ad networks may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA / Google Advertising ID), coarse location derived from IP address, device model and OS version, and ad interaction data to select and measure ads. With basic ads the same networks receive only what is technically required to serve an ad (IP address, device type) and do not use your advertising identifier for personalisation. On Apple devices the networks can only use that identifier if you also allow tracking in the prompt iOS shows; on Android your choice here is the only gate. You can change your choice at any time in Settings inside the game, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Our ad partners process this data under their own policies: Unity / ironSource and Google AdMob. We do not receive personal data from them; we see only aggregated revenue and impression statistics.

Purchases

Purchases (Remove Ads Forever, skins) are handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. The game stores a local record that a purchase was made so it can unlock the item, and can restore it from your store account on a new device.

The support service

RIFT registers a random id made on your device with a small support service run by the publisher, so that if you write in about a purchase we can find your record and give you what you are owed, and so support can send you a gift. We store only a one-way hash of that id, never the id itself, along with your platform and app version. It is not your name, and it cannot be worked back into one.

The service is hosted by Fly.io, Inc. on one encrypted disk in Singapore. Cloudflare, Inc. stores an off-site copy of each daily backup, encrypted on our own server before it is uploaded to a key only the publisher holds, so neither Cloudflare nor the server can read those copies. The bucket's location is reported as Asia-Pacific, which is Cloudflare's placement rather than a legal guarantee, and Cloudflare, Inc. is a United States company.

What we do not do

We do not collect names, e-mail addresses, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal data, and we do not sell data. Your game progress, settings, skins and purchase record are stored on your own device.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We configure our mediation as not child-directed, and it signals that to the ad networks it serves from; each network then applies its own policies.

How long data is kept, and deleting it

Everything on your device: your progress, settings, ads choice and purchase record live in the app's own storage, and deleting the app removes them.

Your online record: use DELETE ACCOUNT in the game's Settings. It clears your identifiers and cancels anything waiting for you, and keeps only an activity trail that holds nothing identifying you. Your progress on the device stays where it is, and purchases can still be restored from your store account. You can also email us and quote the support code shown in Settings.

A deletion is immediate in the live record. Copies survive for a while in backups: our own daily backups keep 14 days, Fly.io's disk snapshots keep 5 days, and the off-site encrypted copies are deleted after 30 days and cannot be removed by hand for the first 7. So a deleted record can remain, encrypted, in an off-site copy for up to about 30 days. There is no automatic expiry for players who never ask.

Ad and purchase data is held by the partners named above under their own retention policies — follow their links to ask them about it, or reset your advertising identifier in your device settings at any time. If you email us about a problem we keep that correspondence only as long as it takes to answer you.
