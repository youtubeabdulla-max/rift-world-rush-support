Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

RIFT: World Rush is an arcade game by AN GAMES. It has no account or sign-in and works offline. This policy explains the two things in the game that involve third parties: advertising and in-app purchases.

Advertising

The free version shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation. LevelPlay selects between several ad networks on our behalf; the networks currently enabled are Unity Ads and Google AdMob. Rewarded ads are only shown when you choose to watch one, and a short ad appears after a round you finish. The one-time Remove Ads Forever purchase removes those between-round ads permanently.

On first launch you choose between personalised ads and basic ads. With personalised ads, the ad networks may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA / Google Advertising ID), coarse location derived from IP address, device model and OS version, and ad interaction data to select and measure ads. With basic ads the same networks receive only what is technically required to serve an ad (IP address, device type) and do not use your advertising identifier for personalisation. On Apple devices the networks can only use that identifier if you also allow tracking in the prompt iOS shows; on Android your choice here is the only gate. You can change your choice at any time in Settings inside the game, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Our ad partners process this data under their own policies: Unity / ironSource and Google AdMob. We do not receive personal data from them; we see only aggregated revenue and impression statistics.

Purchases

Purchases (Remove Ads Forever, skins) are handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. The game stores a local record that a purchase was made so it can unlock the item, and can restore it from your store account on a new device.

What we do not do

We do not collect names, e-mail addresses, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal data, and we do not sell data. Game progress is stored only on your device.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We configure our mediation as not child-directed, and it signals that to the ad networks it serves from; each network then applies its own policies.

How long data is kept, and deleting it

We hold no account for you, so there is nothing on our side to delete: your progress, settings, ads choice and purchase record live in the app's own storage on your device, and deleting the app removes them. Ad and purchase data is held by the partners named above under their own retention policies — follow their links to ask them about it, or reset your advertising identifier in your device settings at any time. If you email us about a problem we keep that correspondence only as long as it takes to answer you.
